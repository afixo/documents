## Where things stand

Afixo is the second version of a university final project, rebuilt as Rust services
behind Cloudflare Workers. As of 2026-08-22 the policy service, the gateway and the
decision engine are complete; authentication, identity, disclosure and audit
persistence are skeletons. Pages that describe unfinished behaviour say so.
See [the report](/reference/report/) for what changed between the versions.
