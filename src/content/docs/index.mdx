## Where things stand

Afixo is the second version of a university final project, rebuilt as Rust services
behind Cloudflare Workers. All six services — the gateway, `auth`, `identity`, `policy`,
`disclosure` and `audit` — are implemented and deployed; sign-in, client credentials,
disclosure decisions and the hash-chained audit log work end to end. What is deliberately
left out is listed on [What is deliberately not done](/architecture/not-done/); see
[the report](/reference/report/) for what changed between the versions and the
[changelog](/reference/changelog/) for when.
